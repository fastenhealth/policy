# Patient-Directed Access Request and Authorization to Release Health Data

I request an electronic copy of all available health and billing information about me for my own access and use. 
My identity information has already been collected and linked to my verified Fasten Health account; I do not need to enter it again here.

This request is directed to all healthcare providers, consumer agencies, hospitals, health systems, laboratories, imaging 
centers, pharmacies, health plans, insurers, claims administrators, health information exchanges, and other organizations 
that maintain records about me and that Fasten Health identifies in fulfilling my request.

**Dates of service:** January 1, 2000, to present.

I request my complete available record for these dates of service, including medical histories, demographic data, diagnoses, 
procedures, clinical and encounter notes, medications and prescription histories, laboratory and pathology results, imaging 
reports and available images, referrals, discharge summaries, insurance and enrollment information, claims, billing and payment 
records, and other information maintained about me in designated record sets.

I expressly request inclusion of sensitive health information, to the extent disclosure is permitted by applicable law, 
including sexually transmitted infections or diseases; HIV/AIDS testing and treatment; mental and behavioral health; 
substance use disorders and treatment; reproductive and sexual health, including contraception, pregnancy, abortion, and 
fertility care; and genetic information.

At my request, for my own access and use, I authorize the record holders described above to disclose the records described 
above to Fasten Health, Inc., and direct them to deliver these records electronically to Fasten Health, Inc., at the secure 
delivery destination specified in the request transmitted to the source, so I can access them through Fasten Connect.

I permit Fasten to use contracted service providers acting on its behalf to locate records, submit and administer my 
request, communicate with record holders, and securely receive and transmit records solely to fulfill it. This permission 
does not authorize unrelated uses or disclosures or appoint Fasten Health, Inc. or its service providers as my legal personal 
representative.

This request covers dates of service from January 1, 2000, through the date I electronically accept this request, not 
ongoing access to future records. Where applicable law requires additional or separate consent, Fasten Health will obtain 
it before using a route that requires it.

I may revoke this authorization at any time in writing by emailing [notices@fastenhealth.com](mailto:notices@fastenhealth.com) or delivering a written 
revocation to the record holder making the disclosure. Fasten Health, Inc. will notify the affected record holders when 
it receives my revocation. Revocation does not affect actions already taken in reliance on this authorization before the 
record holder receives it. I may also cancel an unfulfilled access request through Fasten Health, Inc.

**Expiration:** This authorization expires one year after the date I electronically sign it, unless I revoke it sooner. 
This expiration governs permission to disclose; it does not expand the dates of service requested above.

**Voluntary authorization:** I understand that I do not have to sign this authorization. My treatment, payment for care, 
health plan enrollment, and eligibility for benefits will not be conditioned on my signing this authorization. If I do not 
sign, Fasten may be unable to retrieve records through a route that requires this authorization.

**Potential redisclosure:** Information disclosed under this authorization may be redisclosed by the recipient and may no longer 
be protected by the HIPAA Privacy Rule. Other applicable laws and contractual restrictions may continue to protect it. 
This statement does not grant permission for uses or disclosures prohibited by law.

**Copy:** I am entitled to a copy of this signed authorization, which Fasten Health will make available to me electronically.

By affirmatively accepting and submitting this request and authorization, I intend to electronically sign it using my 
verified identity.
